## Read, close and quota boundaries

Read23 failure keeps the confirmed Data attempt and original publication
identity. At most two actual reads consume that Data's existing copy slots;
a third failed read does not trigger new19 or claim absence. Prepare can retry
within those slots, or explicit close can recover and clean the known owner.
Close waits the entire preparation/read flight, drops private packet views,
then sends exact Data10. A lost successful10 is resolved only by genuine19
Confirm47 retired22; live confirmation preserves the guard and permits a
later cleanup retry. ReleaseBirth2 and Forget1 follow genuine retirement.
A lost Forget acknowledgement retries the same authority-free idempotent
Forget. Released-stage cleanup does not probe a receipt already forgotten.

One caller's close fences publication while preparation is active. Repeated
Python cancellation waits the actual read and cleanup task to settle; it
cannot release Data while a callback still holds its read. Cleanup failures
coalesce while pending and remain retryable. Frame close shares one task across
Data retirement and its entire hook flight. Repeated cancellation waits hook
settlement; successful hooks run once, and a failed hook remains retryable. An independently returned Frame
belongs to its caller; later operation close does not dispose that Frame.

token. After known Data10, genuine retirement, Release and Forget, the19 token
is removed;20 sequential publications are a pressure control at the unchanged

weak receipt tracking shares the existing attempt ownership, without a new
